# Regional_Sales_Analysis

Project: 
US B2B Retail Performance Analytics (2014–2018)This project transforms over 64,000 raw transaction records into a strategic analytical pipeline for a US-based B2B retail giant. By synthesizing data across four geographic regions and three distribution channels, this analysis provides a data-driven blueprint for optimizing a $1.24 billion revenue portfolio.

Executive Summary:

The business currently operates with "blind spots" in product profitability and regional resource allocation. This project bridges the gap by delivering a robust multi-tool workflow—from Python-based Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to Power BI Interactive Dashboards—to convert transactional noise into actionable intelligence.

Key Business Metrics:

Total Revenue: $1,235,968,899 
Total Profit: $461,772,321
Avg. Profit Margin: ~37.4% 
Customer Base: 175 
Corporate Accounts Transaction Volume: 64,104 Line Items 

Project Objectives:

Regional Benchmarking: Identify geographic "hotspots" and underperforming territories across the South, Midwest, West, and Northeast.
Channel Optimization: Quantify the relative profitability of Wholesale (54% of volume), Distributor, and Export channels.
Pareto Analysis: Identify the top 10% of customers driving the majority of revenue to mitigate concentration risk
Time-Series Forecasting: Detect seasonal peaks and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth trends from 2014 to 2018.

Technical Workflow:
* Excel → Data Understanding & Cleaning
* Python → Data Validation & EDA & Feature Engineering
* SQL → KPI Analysis
* Power BI → Visualization & Dashboard

Critical Insights & Constraints:
Budget Gap: Only ~24% of records contain budget data, requiring a subset-specific variance analysis.
Concentration: California represents the most significant geographic footprint with 12,000 transactions.
Portfolio: The analysis tracks 30 distinct products, ranging from $167 to $6,566 in unit price.
